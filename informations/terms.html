The short version

A plain-language summary. The full legal text below is what governs in case of any difference.

What Opac actually does

We run a blind evaluation process between candidates and companies. We are not an employer and cannot guarantee that any application leads to an interview or a job offer.

Your subscription

Recruiter and candidate plans renew automatically until you cancel. Annual plans include a 20% discount and lock in for the year once started.

Playing fair

Recruiters agree not to try to identify anonymized candidates before the reveal stage. Candidates agree to provide accurate information throughout the process.

If something goes wrong

Our liability is limited as described in the Liability section below. You can always reach us through the Contact page or the data request form on the Privacy page.

01

Acceptance of terms

By creating an account or using any part of the Opac platform, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, you should not use the platform. These terms apply equally to candidates and recruiting organizations, referred to together below as "users."

02

Description of the service

Opac operates a blind recruitment platform that connects job candidates with recruiting companies through the structured, identity-masked evaluation process described on our Interviews and Guide pages. Opac is a technology intermediary: we do not employ candidates, and we do not guarantee that any application will result in an interview or a job offer.

03

Accounts and eligibility

You must be at least 16 years old to create a candidate account, and legally authorized to represent your organization to create a recruiter account. You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide and for keeping your login credentials confidential.

04

Candidate obligations

  • Provide accurate, truthful information in your written screening, voice interview and any supporting documents.
  • Do not attempt to reveal your own identity to a recruiter before the reveal stage in a way that would create an unfair advantage.
  • Do not use the platform to apply on behalf of someone else without their consent.
05

Recruiter obligations and non-circumvention

  • Evaluate candidates only on the material made available at each stage, without attempting to identify or de-anonymize a candidate before the reveal stage.
  • Apply the same evaluation criteria to every candidate for a given role.
  • Do not use information obtained through Opac to discriminate on any ground prohibited by Article L1132-1 of the French Labour Code.
06

Subscriptions and billing

  • Recruiter plans (Starter, Business, Enterprise) and the candidate Premium plan are billed monthly or annually, as described on the Pricing page.
  • Annual plans include a 20% discount and renew automatically unless cancelled before the renewal date.
  • You can cancel at any time from your dashboard. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and fees already paid are non-refundable except where required by law.
