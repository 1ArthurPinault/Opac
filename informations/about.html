About Opac

Judged on skills.
Not on who you are.

Opac exists because too many good candidates were being filtered out before anyone read a single line of their application. We build the tools to fix that, one blind interview at a time.

Our mission is simple: make merit the only criterion that matters in a hiring decision, and give every recruiter the tools to prove it.

How we got here

From a frustration shared by three friends to a platform used across 18 countries.

2021

Opac is founded in Paris by three friends who had each seen qualified candidates rejected for reasons that had nothing to do with the job.

2022

Opac runs its first fully blind evaluation process for a mid-size technology company, covering the written screening stage.

2023

The network reaches 50 partner companies, and the voice-modulated interview stage launches to remove gender and age cues from live calls.

2024

Opac raises a Series A round to build the anonymized video panel technology used in final-round interviews.

2026

Today, Opac supports more than 120 companies across 18 countries and 7 industry sectors.

What we stand for

The principles behind every product decision we make.

Fairness by design

Anonymity is not a setting you can turn off. It is built into every stage of the process by default.

Radical transparency

Every statistic and legal reference we publish is sourced. We would rather show fewer numbers than unverified ones.

Data minimalism

We collect only what logistics require, encrypt it, and delete it as soon as it is no longer needed.

Rigor over gut feeling

Structured, skills-first evaluation beats a hunch, every single time we have measured it.
