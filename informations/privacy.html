Privacy policy

Your data, minimized
by design

This page explains what Opac collects, how it is used during the blind evaluation process, how long it is kept, and how to exercise your rights under the RGPD.

Last updated: January 2026

What we collect How we use it Retention & deletion Your rights Submit a request Cookies
01

What we collect

Only what is needed to run the blind evaluation process and handle logistics once you are hired.

Identity data

Name, email and phone number, collected at signup. Encrypted and hidden from recruiters until the reveal stage.

Application data

Your written answers, skills and experience level, shown to recruiters without identity markers.

Voice recordings

Captured during stage 2 interviews, processed through our anonymization filter, then deleted automatically.

Technical data

Login timestamps and basic device information, used only for account security and service reliability.

02

How we use it

Your identity data and application data are handled separately at every stage of the process.

During stages 1 and 2

Recruiters see only your written answers and voice-modulated responses. Name, photo, age and address stay encrypted.

At the reveal stage

If you are made an offer, your identity data is decrypted and shared with the hiring team, and only then.

With companies on Opac

We never sell your data. Companies only receive what is required to evaluate and, if selected, hire you.

03

Retention and deletion

Different types of data are kept for different lengths of time, and never longer than necessary.

Voice recordings

Deleted automatically once the recruitment process for the role you applied to is closed.

Application data

Kept for future roles if you opt in, or deleted at the end of the process if you do not.

Identity data

Kept only as long as your account is active, or as required by law once you are hired.

04

Your rights under the RGPD

The EU General Data Protection Regulation gives you direct control over your personal data.

  • Right of access

    Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

  • Right to rectification

    Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

  • Right to erasure

    Ask us to delete your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions.

  • Right to data portability

    Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format to transfer elsewhere.

  • Right to object and restrict processing

    Object to certain uses of your data, or ask us to limit how it is processed.

05

Submit a data request

We respond to every request within one month, extendable to three months for complex cases, as required by the RGPD.

06

Cookies

Opac uses only the cookies strictly necessary to keep you logged in and to remember your billing preference. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.
