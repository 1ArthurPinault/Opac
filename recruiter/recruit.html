Why recruiters choose Opac

Four concrete reasons hiring teams switch part of their pipeline to blind evaluation.

Reduce unconscious bias

Every candidate is scored on the same blind criteria through the written and voice stages, removing name, photo, age and gender from the equation.

Better quality of hire

Structured, skills-first evaluations help your team compare candidates on what actually predicts performance.

Stronger diversity metrics

Build a pipeline that reflects the market you sell to, not just the network your team already has.

Lower legal & compliance risk

The business case, in numbers

Independent research on diversity, hiring bias and the cost of getting a hire wrong.

25%

Companies in the top quartile for gender diversity on executive teams were 25% more likely to post above-average profitability than those in the bottom quartile.

Source: McKinsey, Diversity Wins, 2019

30%

A bad hire can cost at least 30% of that employee's first-year earnings once recruitment, onboarding and lost productivity are factored in.
