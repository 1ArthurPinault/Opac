Billing questions

What people usually ask before subscribing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly and annual plans can be cancelled at any time from your dashboard. Annual plans simply stop renewing at the end of the current billing period.

What payment methods are accepted?

Major credit cards for individual candidates, plus SEPA direct debit for companies billed in euros.

Is there really a discount for annual billing?

Yes, every recruiter and candidate plan includes a 20% discount when billed annually instead of monthly.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time, and the change takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
