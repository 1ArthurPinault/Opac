The interview process

Three steps. Zero bias.
One fair shot.

Every Opac interview is designed so recruiters evaluate what you can do, not who you are. Here is exactly how it works, why it matters, and what the law says about it.

How an Opac interview unfolds

Depending on the role, you will go through up to three stages. Each one hides a different set of identity cues.

1
20-30 min · written, asynchronous

Blind written screening

You answer a short case study and skills questionnaire. Recruiters only ever see your answers, your skills and your experience level.

  • Evaluated: reasoning, technical answers, writing clarity
  • Hidden: name, photo, age, gender, address
  • Employers renamed as "mid-size tech company", etc.
2
30-45 min · live call

Voice-modulated interview

A live conversation over the Opac platform. Your voice is pitch-normalized in real time and regional accents are softened.

  • Evaluated: motivation, communication, problem-solving
  • Hidden: gender, age bracket, accent, name
  • Recruiter hears a neutral, consistent voice profile
3
45-60 min · final panel

Anonymized video round

For roles with a final culture-fit stage, you join a video call through a soft anonymizing filter. Identity is revealed only after the panel has scored you.

  • Evaluated: team fit, leadership, technical depth
  • Hidden: face, physical appearance, style cues
  • Reveal happens only post-decision, with your consent

The legal framework behind Opac

Our process is inspired by, and goes further than, existing French and European legislation.

Article L1132-1 · Code du travail

The cornerstone of French anti-discrimination law. It prohibits excluding a candidate from a recruitment process based on more than 25 protected criteria, at every stage from hiring to dismissal.

Loi n. 2008-496 du 27 mai 2008

Transposes two EU directives into French law and formally defines direct and indirect discrimination, the legal basis referenced by Article L1132-1.

Loi n. 2006-396 du 31 mars 2006

Introduced the "CV anonyme" obligation for companies with 50+ employees. Its enabling decree was never published, and the requirement became optional under the 2015 law on social dialogue. Opac makes voluntarily, by design, what the law never managed to enforce.

RGPD · Reglement UE 2016/679

Governs how Opac collects, encrypts and eventually reveals your identity data, applying data minimization for as long as your file remains anonymized.

Frequently asked questions

What candidates ask us most before their first interview.

Why do I still give my name and contact details when I sign up?

Opac needs to verify your identity and handle logistics such as contracts once you are hired. That information is encrypted and never shown to recruiters during the written and voice stages. It is only decrypted if a company decides to make you an offer.

Will a recruiter ever see my face or photo?

Only if you reach the final panel round, and even then through an anonymizing filter. Your real image is revealed only after the panel has already scored you and a decision has been reached.

What happens to my voice recording afterwards?

Recordings are automatically deleted once the recruitment process for that role closes. Full details are available on our Privacy page.

Can I skip straight to the written round only?

Some roles are marked as "async-friendly" and can be completed entirely in writing. Look for the badge on the job listing.

I need a disability-related accommodation. What can I do?

Contact our support team from your dashboard. We arrange reasonable adjustments to the format of your interview without breaking anonymity, in line with the disability provisions of the Labour Code.

Is Opac's process legally binding for the companies that recruit through it?

Opac is a private evaluation platform. It is not a substitute for the (currently optional) anonymous CV provisions of French law. Recruiters commit to following our blind evaluation process through their contract with Opac.
