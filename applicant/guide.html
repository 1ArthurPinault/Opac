01

Before you apply

A short checklist to go through before submitting your first application. Check items off as you complete them.

Your email and phone number are used only for logistics, never shown to recruiters.
No name, photo or age. Focus on skills, achievements and concrete numbers.
"Reduced onboarding time by 30%" evaluates better than a job title alone.
A calm environment helps the voice-modulation engine work at its best.
Some roles let you complete the entire process in writing.
Portfolio, certifications or writing samples, if the role asks for them.
02

The three stages, in short

A quick recap of how the interview process works. For the full breakdown, statistics and legal background, see the Interviews page.

Stage 1

Blind written screening

  • Answer a case study in writing
  • No name, photo, age or address shown
  • Focus on clarity and structure
Stage 2

Voice-modulated interview

  • Live call, voice pitch normalized
  • Prepare 2-3 concrete project examples
  • Speak naturally, the filter runs in real time
Stage 3

Anonymized final panel

  • Video call through a soft visual filter
  • Focus on team fit and depth questions
  • Identity revealed only after scoring
03

Your rights as a candidate

Opac's process is built around a simple set of guarantees.

Right to anonymity

Your name, photo, age and address stay hidden through stages 1 and 2, and behind a filter in stage 3, until an offer decision is made.

Right to access and erase your data

Under the RGPD, you can request a copy of your data or ask for it to be deleted at any time. See the Privacy page.

Right to non-discrimination

French law prohibits hiring decisions based on more than 25 criteria, including origin, gender, age and disability. See the legal framework on the Interviews page.

Right to reasonable accommodation

If you need an adapted interview format for a disability, contact support from your dashboard before your first stage.

04

After the interview

What happens once you have completed your stages.

  • Scoring is completed. The recruiting team finishes evaluating every candidate for the role on the same anonymized basis.

  • Identity is revealed, only if you are selected. Your name and contact details are decrypted and shared with the hiring team.

  • The standard hiring process takes over. Contract, onboarding and any remaining logistics happen directly between you and the company, outside Opac.

  • Your data is archived or deleted. Depending on your privacy settings, your anonymized file is either kept for future roles or permanently deleted.

05

Glossary

A few terms you will come across while using Opac.

Blind screening

The first stage of the process, where recruiters review only your written answers, skills and experience level, with no identity markers attached.

Voice modulation

A real-time audio filter that normalizes pitch and softens regional accents during stage 2, so gender and age cues are not audible to the recruiter.

Anonymizing filter

The visual filter applied during stage 3 video calls, replacing your image with a neutral silhouette or avatar until the reveal stage.

Reveal stage

The moment, after a hiring decision has been made, when your real identity is decrypted and shared with the hiring team.

Async-friendly role

A job listing that can be completed entirely through the written stage, without a live voice or video call.

RGPD / GDPR

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (Reglement UE 2016/679), which governs how Opac collects, stores and eventually reveals your personal data.
