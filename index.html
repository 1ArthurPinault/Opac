Hired on skills.
Not on who you are.

Opac hides your name, photo, age and gender from recruiters until you're offered the job. Real companies, real roles, evaluated fairly from the first application to the final interview.

Three steps. Zero bias.

Every application on Opac goes through the same blind evaluation, whichever side of the table you're on.

1

Blind written screening

Candidates answer a case study in writing. Recruiters see only the answer, never a name, photo or age.

2

Voice-modulated interview

A live call with real-time voice normalization, so gender and age cues stay out of the conversation.

3

Anonymized final panel

A video round behind a soft filter. Identity is revealed only after the hiring decision is made.
